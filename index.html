🛡️ Car Insurance - Pay Less

Free ways to cut your premium (from the shop side of things):
  • Shop it every 6-12 months - loyalty rarely gets rewarded. Compare 3+ quotes with the same coverage.
  • Raise the deductible ($500 → $1,000) if you've got the cash set aside.
  • Older paid-off car? Check whether collision is still worth what it costs each year.
  • Ask for every discount: bundle, good driver, defensive-driving course, low mileage, paid-in-full, paperless.
  • Safe-driving apps / plug-in trackers can drop the rate - but they can raise it too if you drive hard.
  • Fix chips in the windshield early - many policies cover the repair for $0.
DG317 isn't a licensed insurance agent and doesn't sell insurance. Quote sites may pay a referral fee; your price is the same either way.
